# Gateway people walkthrough

Gateway roster stories are **Components/Gateway people**: **People**, **Empty**,
**Loading**, **Unavailable and retry**, **Denied and hidden**, **No serving Gateway**,
and **Avatar fallback**. Refresh the failed read, check member/maintainer labels,
and repeat at phone width. These fixtures do not establish native profiles or
identity-provider admission; use the installed Gateway proof for that boundary.


1. Build Storybook from the candidate source and open **Components/Gateway people / People**.
2. Capture the desktop roster and the same card at 390px. Verify both names and role labels.
3. Open **Unavailable and retry**. Select **Refresh people** and verify the two profiles return.
4. Inspect **Empty**, **Loading**, **Denied and hidden**, **No serving Gateway**, and **Avatar fallback**.
5. Record the refresh/recovery interaction. Label these captures as simulated UI proof.
6. Repeat the populated roster and avatar checks against installed OCC after two real humans connect.

Keep captures outside the checkout and attach screenshots and a short walkthrough to the PR.
